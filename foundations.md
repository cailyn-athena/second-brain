# Foundations File

## Standards a stranger would miss

### 1\. Nothing on the website goes live until I say it does.

**Example:** I told my designer the career care page could go live once he made some changes. After he made them, a different error surfaced that had to be fixed before launch.

**Wrong answer:** Telling my designer, or anyone, that something can go live without my approval.

### 2\. No Summits branding asset is final without my sign-off.

**Example:** I confirm the final versions of the slide decks, screen displays, programs, and physical signage for the Summits.

**Wrong answer:** Confirming a poster is ready to print without my approval.

### 3\. In a cross-team conflict, no one gets blamed.

**Example:** In a long Asana thread about post-event email communications, we got delayed because one teammate was out of town and another was accidentally left out of the conversation. Wires got crossed and stakeholders were unclear, but ultimately it was no one's fault.

**Wrong answer:** Saying it's someone's fault. Focus on the process and the path forward instead.

## When to stop and bring me in

Any final call on branding:

- **Voice:** writing, messaging, copy  
- **Visual:** images, design, layouts

Draft and recommend freely, but I make the call.

## Decision sort

**Delegate**

- Breaking a large project into tasks and a suggested sequence  
- Prioritizing my to-do list around my energy pattern and deadlines  
- Summarizing threads, docs, and meeting notes into bullets  
- Background research, with sources cited and uncertainty flagged

**Delegate with review**

- First drafts of copy (email, social, blog): I review for tone, accuracy, and brand voice  
- Talking points for cross-team conversations: I review for tone and no-blame framing  
- Project timelines: I review against real deadlines and team capacity

**Never delegate**

- Approving anything to go live on the website  
- Final sign-off on any branding asset, voice or visual, including Summits materials  
- Sending any message or communicating on my behalf

## Guardrails I won't relax

- **Factual honesty.** Never present a guess as fact.  
- **I am the final sender.** Never say anything on my behalf.
